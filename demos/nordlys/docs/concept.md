# Nordlys GP: 2027 season launch

Built with the stagecraft skill as a demonstration. Nordlys GP is a fictional Formula 1 team.

**Concept.** A season launch that reads like a timing screen: every chapter is a sector of the
story, and the scroll is the throttle.

**Direction.** "Telemetry Poster": Poster (condensed caps at poster scale, black, paper, one team
colour, tilted poster wall, digits that scramble in) remixed with Specimen (mono readouts, grid
ticks, numbered sections, a spec sheet).

**Hero.** The car number set in type at poster scale beside the statement: the number is identity, not the subject, so it stays typographic. The subject is the lap, and the lap is the signature.

**Signature moment.** The Lap: a pinned stage where a hand-drawn circuit draws itself, then a
marker drives the lap as you scroll while sector times, speed and gear read out live.
Everything else is quieter to protect it.

**Intake defaults taken.** Launch or campaign; a developer maintains it; Expressive motion (no 3D,
no video: the team has no photography yet, which is the one ingredient this site is missing and
is laid out to receive); feel chosen from the shortlist. Stack: Astro, static output, GSAP +
ScrollTrigger + MotionPath, Lenis on fine pointers only. Stated, not asked.

**Palette.** Ink `#0b0b0d`, paper `#f2efe6`, aurora `#6cff8c` (blocks and rules only on paper, text
on ink), warm grey `#b9b6ad`. **Type.** Anton (display), Archivo variable (text), JetBrains Mono
variable (telemetry).

**Chapters.** Statement (number, name, three live stats), marquee, The Lap (signature), The car
(spec sheet), Drivers (index), Calendar (24 rounds, rail that draws), Results (table), Poster
wall, Partners (proof marquee with pause), Join the grid (resolution), Ending.
