Ledger

ClientFintech
Year2026
RoleDesign engineer
DurationEleven weeks

Problem

Statements were a wall of rows; people could not find one payment.

Role

Design engineer, with one designer and two back-end engineers, eleven weeks.

Craft

A timeline grouped by day with a sticky date rail, amounts set in tabular figures, category colour as a thin edge rather than a chip, a search that filters as you type without layout shift, keyboard navigation through rows, and reduced-motion variants of every transition.

Outcome

Support tickets about "finding a payment" dropped by half in the first month, measured by the client's support team.
