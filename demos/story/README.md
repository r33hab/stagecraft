# Halde 8

Demo built with the stagecraft skill (see docs/concept.md). The story of a fictional 1958 cine camera,
told by the camera: a live three.js render of "Vintage Video Camera" by Jenelle van Heerden and
James Ray Cock (Poly Haven, CC0) in a Poly Haven studio HDRI (CC0), `src/lib/kino/stage.ts`;
`public/kino/` holds the model split into its parts for the exploded view
(`node ../../scripts/explode-gltf.mjs ../../site/public/kino/camera.gltf public/kino/camera-parts.gltf --spread 0.84,0.84,1`),
the light and a still of every chapter's shot.
