# Changelog

## 2026-11-09
### Added
- `references/production.md` and step 1b of the build flow: for Cinematic, 3D and award briefs the skill now sets a bar (a board of reference frames), picks a production route per scene (real or generated models, offline or generated footage, live WebGL only for what the visitor operates), asks for the access that route needs, and stops at two gates (one style frame, one finished scene) before building the rest. Written after a day lost to a procedural WebGL world that never reached its references.
- `scripts/board.mjs`: `capture` saves full-size frames of a public site scrolled by wheel; `pair` puts your frame beside a reference frame so the gap is judged on one image.
- Intake Q9 (which sites should it stand next to) and Q10 (what the scroll does: travels between scenes, turns one object, plays a film), and a tools-and-access step that names what a route needs before any code.
- Critique: a Bar axis, a board pairing in the review pass, two more blocking questions (does it hold up next to the board; was the reported frame re-captured), a Weakest line in the delivery note, and production anti-patterns (worlds built from code, quality judged from thumbnails, self-praise, silent fallbacks, video seeked per scroll event, clips that take the scroll away, bare cuts from footage to live 3D, NaN under bloom).
- Eval `cinematic-world-no-assets`: a 3D brief with no material must lead to a bar, a route and an access request, not to code.
### Fixed
- perf spec: font files requested from `@font-face` have initiator type `css` and were counted as CSS weight; the font check runs first now. Copied to the site and the demos.

## 2026-11-03
### Added
- `references/craft.md`: the mechanics of directed sites as numbers: one carrier through the chapters, chapter changes by a moving edge, text reveal families with hold minimums, pins and two horizontal forms (stepped track, rail with depth), loaders that become the hero, one easing signature, 2D/3D craft that holds up on phones, the pointer layer, page transitions, pace.
- `reading.spec.ts`: scrolls each route at a steady reading pace and fails copy that is gone before it held long enough to read, copy in view that never becomes readable, empty screens between chapters, and copy stacked on copy (`reading` block in the budgets file).
- `scripts/frames.mjs`: films a page at reading pace on the real GPU and writes contact sheets, a text timeline and a reading verdict; the critique step now watches the site frame by frame.
- Landing: the docked frame plays a reel of the example sites (filmed loops, one at a time, loaded only once the frame docks) beside a list of the three sites with thumbnails; each door swaps in a real preview: the build replayed step by step, the sixteen directions on a turning ring, the suite's measurements drawn over the page they measured, both builds of one brief with their numbers. Replaces the grey placeholder previews.
- `craft.md` section 11, showing the work (indexes, previews and reels, with numbers), and the matching anti-pattern in the critique.
- `craft.md`: choose a subject that renders (hard-surface objects under captured studio light; never a natural phenomenon as a live hero), a real model under real light, the same object as its own ink drawing with a screen-space wipe shared by model, background and split-colour copy, labels projected from points on the model, and the camera move into the object; `stack.md` "A product on a studio set" with assets, loading, shots and measured cost; a load-after-tier-check rule in `performance.md`; anti-patterns for flat or tilting dives, phenomena as heroes, scenery with no hand and the italic-serif accent headline.
- Intake Q8, where people will mostly see it (computer, everywhere, phones): it sets `"platform"` in the budgets file; `"desktop-first"` makes the mobile perf budgets warn while accessibility, reading and layout still gate.
- `scripts/explode-gltf.mjs`: splits a glTF model into its connected parts for an exploded view and writes each part's axis, distance and order (layers by what covers what, outermost first, glass riding with its holder) into its node extras; `craft.md` (order, framing the spread, inside faces, labels that ride with the parts), `stack.md` (the runtime) and choreography move 5 explain how to play it.
- Story demo: "Taken apart" dismantles the lit camera into its 39 parts and wipes them into an exploded drawing with numbered labels on the parts; "Rebuilt" puts it back together in the drawing before the camera closes in and the light wipes back. - `film.mjs --drive`: a module drives the page while filming (hover rows, switch views) when a scroll would never show the signature; notes on filming at the frame's ratio and on H.264 not playing in Playwright's bundled Chromium.

## 2026-11-02
### Added
- Initial release: SKILL.md flow (intake, concept, stack, tokens, story, build order, test, critique), the reference files, runtime helpers (tier, reveal, lenis-gsap, motion.css), a five-project Playwright suite with budgets, and the add-tests and check-budgets scripts.
- Field notes on techniques seen on public high-end sites, paired with their runtime facts.
- `references/choreography.md`: twenty-one choreographed moves with mechanics (vertical-to-horizontal handoff, frame to full bleed, stage swaps with the reading-time rule, camera dolly, disassembly, logo as window, pre-rendered video objects, designed ending, one object carried through every chapter) and the first-second hooks.
- Intake questions 5 to 7: the one thing worth showing (the signature is made of the subject, never an ornament), how people move through the site (one scroll, a landing that routes into pages, chapters as pages), and what there is to show (the asset floor). Four questions is the size of one round, not a cap.
- A quality floor for ambitious briefs in SKILL.md, a motion review step (copy hold time, hard cuts, flat 3D, ornaments, nothing in the frame) and a page-map section in `references/ux.md`.
- Step 1 now mines the subject's world (trade, objects, rituals, vocabulary) before any concept, so the visual system comes from the subject; the generic product page is listed as an anti-pattern; example sites flow with the main site through a shared cross-document view transition and a way back; a lighting change joins the first-second hooks.
- Sourcing assets in `references/stack.md`: CC0 scans, textures and HDRIs (Poly Haven API needs a User-Agent), licence rules for a public repo, glTF optimisation, offline renders with headless Blender; a 3D subject is never built from primitives.
- `scripts/film.mjs`: films any page on the real GPU as seamless H.264 loops at two sizes plus a poster, with a text-free option for clips that sit behind copy.
- `assets/runtime/hero-scene.ts`: loads a real GLB (Draco and meshopt) under studio light with a contact shadow, turntable, parallax and scroll turn and dolly; `softwareGl()` in `tier.ts` keeps scenes off software renderers.
- anime.js 4.5 in `references/stack.md` (springs, physics drag, SVG draw and morph, grid stagger, text split, scopes) with recipes in `references/motion.md`; one engine per site.
- The perf spec prints `scroll length (screens)` per route and warns past 12; `budgets.scrollScreens` makes it fail.
- `site/`: the skill's GitHub Pages site, built with the skill: a landing that routes into Method, Directions, Suite and Compare pages.
- `demos/`: three briefs built twice by the same model, prompt and guidelines, with the skill (nordlys, portfolio, story) and without it (the `-baseline` folders), measured by the same suite; `site/scripts/compare-metrics.mjs` reads the logs into the comparison table.
### Changed
- Custom cursors replace the native cursor and track it 1:1 by setting the transform on `pointermove`; interactive elements grow the dot, form fields restore the native cursor, Tab hands it back to keyboard users.
- Stages use sticky spacers instead of GSAP pins (a pin spacer inserted after first paint shifted the layout by a full pin distance); stage chapters stay in the accessibility tree (opacity, not visibility) and focus scrolls the stage to them.
- The testing reference explains how to check WebGL on the real GPU (new headless Chromium with Metal or ANGLE); the performance reference covers intros and LCP, and video delivery.
- The reveal and stage rules in `motion.md`, the `never stuck` spec (stage-aware), the keyboard spec (reveal grace), the responsive spec (clipped ancestors), the perf spec (fallback faces excluded), and the Lighthouse CI upload target (filesystem).
